## Overview

KITTI-CARLA was built with **CARLA 0.9.10** using a virtual vehicle whose LiDAR and camera layout follows the KITTI acquisition platform: a roof-mounted Velodyne HDL-64 and two color cameras similar to Point Grey Flea 2 cameras.

The dataset targets LiDAR/image **semantic segmentation**, LiDAR/image **odometry**, synthetic-vs-real comparison, and **transfer learning** from CARLA to real KITTI data. Seven sequences of 5,000 frames were generated in seven CARLA maps spanning city, suburban, mountain, rural and highway environments.

<div class="callout"><strong>December 2021 corrected release</strong>The legacy site notes that the dataset was regenerated after correcting a precision loss in the depth images. The new archive should therefore host that corrected version.</div>
